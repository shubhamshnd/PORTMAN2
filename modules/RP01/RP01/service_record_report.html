What this sheet contains

Every Approved service record from SRV01 — record number, service, who it bills to, date, billable quantity, and whether it has been billed. Drafts and pending records are excluded: they are still being filled in.

SRV01's custom fields differ per service type, so the sheet has two shapes:

  • All service types — every approved record, with the custom fields collapsed into one readable Details column.
  • One service type — only that type's records, with its custom fields as real columns you can sort, filter and pivot on.
